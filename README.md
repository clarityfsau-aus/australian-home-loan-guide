## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### How much deposit do I need?

Most lenders require a deposit, although the exact amount varies depending on the lender, loan type, and your financial situation.

### What is Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI)?

LMI is generally required when borrowing a high percentage of a property's value.

### Can I refinance my mortgage?

Yes. Refinancing may help reduce repayments or provide access to additional loan features.
